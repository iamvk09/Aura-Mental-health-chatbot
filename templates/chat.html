About AURA

AURA (Artificial Understanding & Resilience Agent) is an offline mental health chatbot powered by advanced RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and NLP (Natural Language Processing) technologies.

Features:

  • 100% Offline - Your data stays private and secure
  • Encrypted Conversations - All chats are encrypted for your privacy
  • Mental Health Assessment - Personalized assessment on login
  • RAG-Powered Responses - Context-aware, empathetic responses
  • Conversation History - Access your chat history anytime
  • Crisis Detection - Automatic detection and support for crisis situations

How It Works:

AURA uses semantic search to find relevant information from a curated mental health knowledge base, then generates empathetic, supportive responses tailored to your needs.

Privacy & Security:

All your conversations are encrypted using your password. Your data is stored locally and never shared with external services.

Important Note:

AURA is not a replacement for professional mental health care. If you're in crisis, please use the SOS button or contact emergency services immediately.
